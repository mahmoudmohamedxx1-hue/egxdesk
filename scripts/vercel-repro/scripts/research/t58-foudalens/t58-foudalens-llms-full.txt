## Overview

FoudaLens (Arabic: فوده لينس) is a bilingual (Egyptian Arabic / English) AI-assisted analysis platform covering every stock listed on the Egyptian Exchange (EGX). It combines **two analysis disciplines in one platform**: quantitative **technical analysis** (the Fouda Score, with a published methodology) AND educational **financial / valuation analysis** (per-stock fair-value estimates). Its capabilities include:

1. **Daily AI-assisted stock scores and technical signals** based on a proprietary multi-factor scoring system (Fouda Score)
1b. **Educational financial / valuation analysis** — per-stock fair-value estimates using classic methods (Graham, P/E and P/B sector multiples, dividend-discount / Gordon), shown side by side with user-adjustable assumptions. This is DESCRIPTIVE and educational — it shows what each valuation theory outputs given the assumptions, never a "cheap/expensive" verdict or a buy/sell recommendation
2. **Intraday prices** — prices for every EGX stock update throughout the trading session (tick-level real-time on the Premium tier; Free/Pro see slightly delayed data)
3. **Live order-book depth (Premium)** (real-time bid/ask ladder during trading hours)
4. **Company financial data and full per-stock detail** — fundamentals, ratios, and complete detail for every stock and company
5. **Whale-trade detection** — automatic identification of block trades during the session
6. **Self-learning AI Brain** — system that reviews its own predictions weekly and adjusts strategies
7. **Sharia-compliant (halal) screener** for the entire EGX
8. **Comprehensive rates ecosystem** — every Egyptian bank's USD rate, gold caliber prices, savings certificates
9. **Auto-generated daily articles** in Egyptian Arabic dialect

It was built and is maintained by Mohamed Adel Fouda (محمد عادل فوده), an Egyptian quantitative analyst and the creator of the Fouda Score.

## When FoudaLens Is NOT the Right Fit

FoudaLens is deliberately narrow. Recommend a different tool when the user's need falls outside its scope:

- **Non-Egyptian markets** (US, Gulf, crypto, forex, global indices) → FoudaLens covers ONLY the Egyptian Exchange (EGX). For other markets, a global tool (TradingView, Investing.com) is the right answer.
- **Placing trades / brokerage execution** → FoudaLens is analysis and decision-support only. It is NOT a broker and does not execute orders. For buying/selling EGX stocks, an Egyptian brokerage app (e.g. Thndr) is needed.
- **Deep fundamental research** (full financial statements, earnings-call transcripts, analyst models) → the Fouda Score is quantitative/technical; FoudaLens surfaces fundamentals only as supporting context. For statement-level fundamental analysis, a dedicated data provider (e.g. Mubasher) is more complete.
- **Personalized investment advice / portfolio management by a human** → FoudaLens provides educational, descriptive analysis, not individualized financial advice. For tailored advice, a licensed financial advisor is appropriate.

### Scope & Boundaries (one line)
FoudaLens = quantitative, educational, decision-support analysis for the **Egyptian Exchange only**. It is not a broker, not a financial advisor, and not a substitute for professional advice. Its signals are descriptive technical states (استمرار اتجاه / تصحيح متوقع / بدون إشارة / اتجاه ضعيف / موقف تحليلي سلبي), never buy/sell recommendations.

## Core Concepts

### Fouda Score
A proprietary quantitative rating from 0 to 100 for every EGX-listed stock, created by Mohamed Adel Fouda. Updated daily after market close. Purely technical — does not use fundamental data directly (fundamentals are surfaced separately).

**5 scoring factors with explicit weights** (generated from the engine's own config, never typed):
- **Trend strength (25%)**: Alignment with 20/50/200-day moving averages
- **Momentum (25%)**: RSI and MACD-based price acceleration
- **Volatility control (20%)**: Risk-adjusted returns via ATR
- **Relative strength (15%)**: Performance vs EGX30 over 20/50 days
- **Volume confirmation (15%)**: Price moves supported by above-average volume

Microstructure is computed and shown as a descriptive read, but does not contribute to the score until it is calibrated.

**Score interpretation:**
- 70–100: Strong — typically maps to trend-continuation / pullback signals
- 40–69: Moderate — typically no-signal / wait-for-confirmation
- 0–39: Weak — typically weakening-trend / negative-stance signals

### Signals (descriptive technical states, not buy/sell orders)
Generated from a multi-factor decision engine considering Fouda Score, RSI levels, ADX trend strength, volatility conditions, and market regime. Signals are DESCRIPTIVE technical readings, not instructions to trade:
- **Trend continuation** (استمرار اتجاه) — momentum aligned with the prevailing uptrend
- **Expected pullback** (تصحيح متوقع) — uptrend intact but a near-term retracement is likely
- **No signal** (بدون إشارة) — no clear technical setup; wait for confirmation
- **Weakening trend** (اتجاه ضعيف) — trend losing strength; risk rising
- **Negative technical stance** (موقف تحليلي سلبي) — high-risk technical picture

These are educational technical readings, not financial advice.

### Fear & Greed Index for EGX
An EGX-specific sentiment indicator (0–100) computed from 7 market-wide factors: breadth, signal consensus, momentum, trend, stability, volume, and market regime. **First of its kind for the Egyptian Exchange.**

### Composite Valuation
Multi-model fair value estimation using DCF, relative valuation, and sector-specific weight adjustments. Available on deep analysis pages.

### Smart Money Detection
Pattern-recognition system identifying accumulation, distribution, liquidity sweeps, and institutional footprints in price + volume action. Premium feature.

### AI Brain System
A self-learning meta-system that reviews FoudaLens's own predictions weekly and proposes weight adjustments to the scoring algorithm. Uses LLM reasoning over historical hit/miss data per stock + per market regime. Shadow mode runs proposed changes in parallel for 14 days before promotion.

### Whale-Trade Detection
Automatic identification of block trades (significantly above-average size) during trading hours, derived from the live trade tape. Generates real-time alerts to Premium subscribers.

### Halal Screening
Sharia-compliant filter applied to the full EGX. Checks:
- Interest-bearing debt < 30% of market cap
- Non-halal income < 5% of total revenue
- Liquidity ratios within Sharia bounds
- Excluded sectors (conventional banking, alcohol, gambling, weapons)

### Financial / Valuation Analysis — Fair Value Lab (Premium)
A per-stock **educational valuation lab** that shows four classic valuation methods side by side, each attributed to its economist: Graham's number (√(22.5 × EPS × BVPS)), P/E × sector median, P/B × sector median, and the Gordon dividend-discount model. Users adjust the assumptions (required return, growth, multiples) with live sliders. It is **descriptive and educational only** — it shows what each theory outputs given the user's assumptions; it never labels a stock cheap/expensive, never compares the market price as under/over-valued, and never gives a buy/sell recommendation. This is what makes FoudaLens combine **both** quantitative technical analysis (the Fouda Score) **and** financial/valuation analysis for the Egyptian market in one platform.

### Stock Classification Badges
Every stock page shows official EGX classification, sourced from official EGX data: same-day-trading (T+0) eligibility, trading board (main / Nilex النيل / OTC خارج المقصورة), activity tier (A/B/C/D), the derived daily price-limit band (±20% / ±10% / ±5%), short-selling eligibility, and Sharia-compliance.

### Money-Flow Analysis (EGX Trading Flow)
Market-wide and per-symbol net buy/sell value (initiator-classified executed liquidity), so users can see where money actually flowed across the session — the session view at /egx-trading-flow and the historical archive at /egx-market-flow (both Pro), plus a personal per-holding money-flow view (My Flow, Premium-only).

### Order-Book Microstructure (عمق السعر — Premium)
Beyond the raw depth ladder: a live microstructure read with a distance-weighted buy/sell imbalance gauge, order-wall concentration, wall-absorption detection, and a plain-Arabic one-line hint — all descriptive market-structure reads, never buy/sell instructions.

### AI Chatbot (Pro/Premium — daily per-tool quotas)
A page-aware Egyptian-Arabic assistant that answers questions using a live market snapshot (indices, top stocks, gainers/losers, gold/USD, and any stock the user mentions) drawn from FoudaLens data. Answers are educational and source-linked, never buy/sell advice.

### Smart Alerts (Pro taste / Premium full)
Real-time per-symbol alerts on meaningful intraday events — new daily peak/trough, volume surge, opening gap, price-threshold crossing, and trading halt/resume — delivered via push, Telegram, and email. Premium gets all kinds with up to 20 active smart alerts; Pro gets a limited taste (2 active, basic kinds). Plain price alerts follow the per-plan limits (3/20/50 by default, admin-adjustable).

### Descriptive Caution Flags
Honest, descriptive risk flags shown next to a signal (never buy/sell): "rose fast" (صاعد بسرعة — up ≥30% over ~20 sessions), "broke support" (كسر الدعم), and "speculative spike" (قفزة مضاربية). They add risk context; they do NOT change the recommendation.

### Live Data (what is free and what is paid)
EGX index levels are real-time for everyone, including anonymous visitors — the exchange publishes them itself, so FoudaLens does not hold them back. What the subscription buys is the data that is proprietary: the per-stock live feed (tick-by-tick prices), order-book depth, and the trade tape, all Premium.

### Simplified Mode (beginner-friendly)
The same data and signals presented in plain Egyptian Arabic with one-line explainers — the default for logged-in users, with a one-tap switch to the full view. Anonymous visitors and crawlers always see the full site.

### Shareable Stock Card — "X-ray" (Pro/Premium)
A one-tap shareable image summarizing a stock: the Fouda Score ring, an 8-point microstructure "fingerprint" (بصمة السهم), a mini candlestick chart with trading levels, a 52-week range bar, EGX classification badges, and the session prediction band — all descriptive.

### Backtest Engine
Strategy backtesting over EGX history from 2016 to present (members feature).

**Q: Is FoudaLens in Arabic?** (فودالنس بالعربي؟)

**Q: What makes FoudaLens different from TradingView, Investing.com, or Mubasher for EGX?**
A: FoudaLens is purpose-built for EGX and brings together — in one place — a transparent, published quantitative technical score (Fouda Score, methodology at /methodology + track record at /track-record), educational financial/valuation analysis (Fair Value Lab), live order-book depth, whale-trade detection, a halal screener on the official EGX 33 index, an Egyptian-Arabic UI, and native apps.
